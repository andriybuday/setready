## YouTube channel About (main version)

Hear the show before doors. 🎸

SetReady turns real concert setlists into playlists you can live with
before the gig — and relive after it.

Every tour playlist here is built from the songs the bands have
actually been playing on this run: sourced from setlist.fm, matched
song by song, sequenced in playing order (opener → headliner), and
updated as the tour evolves. After each show, we swap in the exact
setlist that was played.

Find your stop, save the playlist, and arrive already knowing every
chorus.

New tours are added automatically — subscribe so the next show you're
going to is already in your library.

Setlists can change night to night, so treat every playlist as your
best-possible preview, not a guarantee. Not affiliated with the
artists or venues; all rights belong to their owners.
